# Five-Day Gym Program: Muscle, Athleticism, and Calisthenics

## Monday: Upper A: Push, Shoulders, and Planche

Build pressing strength and upper-body muscle without exhausting yourself before Ultimate. Hard stop at 65 minutes.

Target duration: 60 minutes

+ Ultimate practice later

### Upper Body Preparation (warmup)

- Wrist Preparation: 1 x 120s
- Band Pull-Apart: 2 x 15 | rest: isolation
- Scapular Push-Up: 2 x 10-15 | rest: calisthenics skill

### Planche Foundation (calisthenics)

> Emphasize locked elbows, active scapular protraction, posterior pelvic tilt, and quality over fatigue.

- Planche Lean: 3 x 10-20s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Hollow-Body Hold: 2 x 20-30s hold | rest: calisthenics skill

### Primary Strength (strength)

- Incline Dumbbell Press: 3 x 6-10 | rest: moderate compound
- Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound

### Hypertrophy Accessories (strength)

- Cable Fly: 2 x 10-15 | superset: A
- Face Pull / Reverse Cable Fly: 2 x 15-20 | superset: A | rest: isolation | notes: pull to eye height; keep the movement controlled
- Cable Lateral Raise: 3 x 12-20 | superset: B
- Overhead Cable Triceps Extension: 3 x 10-15 | superset: B | rest: isolation

## Tuesday: Lower A: Power, Strength, and Abs

The scheduled run happens separately. This gym session contains no running, sprinting, accelerations, or treadmill work. Hard stop at 65 minutes.

Target duration: 60 minutes

### Non-Running Lower-Body Preparation (warmup)

- Dynamic Warm-Up: Non-running hip, groin, glute, and ankle preparation. (~5-6 min)
  - Adductor rock backs
  - Leg swings
  - Bodyweight glute bridges
  - Ankle dorsiflexion rocks
  - Progressive warm-up sets before Hack Squat

### Jump Power (power)

> Use maximal intent with controlled landings. Stop if jump height or distance clearly declines.

- Box Jump: 3 x 3 | rest: jump
- Standing Broad Jump: 2 x 3 | rest: jump

### Primary Strength (strength)

- Hack Squat: 3 x 6-10 | rest: heavy compound
- Romanian Deadlift: 3 x 6-10 | rest: heavy compound
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 2 x 8-12 each side | rest: moderate compound

### Lower-Body Accessories (strength)

- Seated Leg Curl or Lying Leg Curl: 2 x 10-15 | superset: A
- Adductor Machine: 2 x 12-15 | superset: A | rest: isolation | notes: use a controlled range of motion
- Standing Calf Raise: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation

### Core (core)

- Cable Crunch: 3 x 8-15 | rest: isolation | notes: use spinal flexion rather than pulling with the arms

## Wednesday: Upper B: Pull, Back, and Handstand

Prioritize handstand quality and back development without creating excessive fatigue before Ultimate. Hard stop at 65 minutes.

Target duration: 60 minutes

+ Ultimate practice later

### Handstand Preparation (warmup)

- Wrist Preparation: 1 x 90-120s
- Wall Slide: 1 x 12 | rest: isolation
- Scapular Push-Up: 1 x 12 | rest: calisthenics skill

### Handstand Skill (calisthenics)

> Keep every hold deliberate. Stop before wrist or shoulder fatigue changes your position.

- Wall-Facing Handstand Hold: 3 x 20-40s hold | rest: calisthenics skill

### Primary Pulling Strength (strength)

- Pull-Up: 4 x 3-6 | rest: heavy compound
- Chest-Supported Row: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown: 2 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound

### Back and Arm Accessories (strength)

- Reverse Pec Deck: 3 x 12-20 | superset: A
- Incline Dumbbell Curl: 3 x 8-12 | superset: A | rest: isolation
- Cable Curl: 2 x 12-15 | rest: isolation

### Core (core)

- Ab Wheel: 3 x 6-12 | rest: isolation

## Thursday: Recovery, Mobility, and Calisthenics

This is a low-fatigue gym session that should leave you feeling better before Ultimate, not exhausted.

Target duration: 50 minutes

+ Ultimate practice later

### Joint Preparation (warmup)

- Mobility Flow: Easy joint preparation with no running or conditioning. (~5 min)
  - Controlled hip circles
  - Shoulder circles
  - Gentle wrist preparation

### Mobility (mobility)

- 90/90 Hip Switch: 2 x 6 each side
- Adductor Rock Back: 2 x 8 each side
- Ankle Dorsiflexion Rock: 2 x 10 each side
- Hip Flexor Stretch: 2 x 30s hold, each side
- Open Book Rotation: 1 x 8 each side

### Calisthenics Skills (calisthenics)

> Use the hardest progression you can control cleanly. Keep at least one technically strong attempt in reserve.

- L-Sit Practice: Four high-quality attempts of 8 to 20 seconds using the hardest controlled progression. | rest: calisthenics skill
- Planche Lean: 2 x 10-20s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Scapular Pull-Up: 2 x 8-10 | rest: calisthenics skill
- Dead Hang: 2 x 30-60s hold | rest: calisthenics skill

### Lower-Body Resilience (recovery)

> Keep every set controlled and comfortably short of failure.

- Short-Lever Copenhagen Plank: 2 x 15-30s hold, each side | rest: calisthenics skill
- Single-Leg Glute Bridge: 2 x 10 each side | rest: isolation
- Tibialis Raise: 2 x 15-20 | superset: A
- Standing Calf Raise: 2 x 12-20 | superset: A | rest: isolation

## Friday: Upper C: Shoulders, Back, Arms, and Handstand

Develop the upper-body priority muscles while avoiding meaningful leg fatigue before Saturday's long run. The scheduled run happens separately. Hard stop at 65 minutes.

Target duration: 60 minutes

### Handstand Preparation (warmup)

- Wrist Preparation: 1 x 120s
- Wall Slide: 1 x 12 | rest: isolation

### Handstand Skill (calisthenics)

> Focus on balance and line rather than accumulating fatigue.

- Wall-Facing Handstand Hold: 2 x 30-45s hold | rest: calisthenics skill

### Primary Strength (strength)

- Machine Shoulder Press: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Dip: 3 x 6-10 | rest: moderate compound
- Seated Cable Row: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound

### Shoulder and Arm Accessories (strength)

- Cable Lateral Raise: 3 x 12-20 | superset: A
- Rear-Delt Fly: 3 x 15-20 | superset: A | rest: isolation
- Cable Curl / Dumbbell Curl: 2 x 10-15 | superset: B
- Triceps Pressdown: 2 x 10-15 | superset: B | rest: isolation

### Core (core)

- Hanging Knee Raise or Hanging Leg Raise: 3 x 8-15 | rest: isolation
