# Athletic Muscle Base — Block 1

## Monday: Upper Push + Planche

Build chest, shoulders, and triceps while developing the straight-arm strength and body control needed for long-term planche progression. Ultimate practice later means there is no additional conditioning today.

Target duration: 70 minutes

+ Ultimate practice later

### Upper Body Warm-Up (warmup)

- Upper Body Preparation: Prepare wrists, shoulders, scapulae, and pressing pattern. (~6-8 min)
  - Wrist circles and gentle wrist loading
  - Arm circles
  - Band pull-aparts
  - Scapular push-ups
  - Light incline push-ups

### Planche Foundation (calisthenics)

> Skill work happens while fresh. Prioritize locked elbows, active scapular protraction, posterior pelvic tilt, and excellent position. Never turn this into conditioning.

- Planche Lean: 4 x 10-20s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: locked elbows; push the floor away; gradually increase forward lean
- Hollow-Body Hold: 2 x 20-30s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: keep lower back pressed toward the floor

### Strength (strength)

> Most working sets should finish around 1-3 RIR. Control the eccentric and prioritize excellent technique over load.

- Incline Dumbbell Press: 3 x 6-10 | rest: moderate compound | notes: controlled eccentric; use a comfortable full range of motion
- Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound | notes: keep torso stable; do not excessively arch the lower back
- Cable Fly: 2 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: control the stretch; bring upper arms across the body rather than simply moving the hands
- Cable Lateral Raise: 4 x 12-20 | rest: isolation | notes: lead with the upper arm; control the lowering phase; do not swing
- Overhead Cable Triceps Extension: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: allow a controlled stretch; keep upper arms relatively stable
- Reverse Cable Fly / Face Pull: 2 x 15-20 | rest: isolation | notes: controlled reps; emphasize rear shoulders and upper back


## Tuesday: Acceleration + Lower A + Abs

The primary dedicated speed session of the week. Sprint while fresh, then build lower-body muscle and strength. Sprint quality matters far more than fatigue.

Target duration: 75 minutes

### Athletic Warm-Up (warmup)

- Dynamic Sprint Warm-Up: Progress from easy movement into sprint preparation without fatiguing yourself. (~8-10 min)
  - Easy jog or bike
  - Leg swings
  - Adductor rockbacks
  - Ankle rocks
  - Glute bridges
  - A-skips
  - Pogos
  - Two progressive accelerations

### Acceleration (speed)

> Full recovery between repetitions. Every rep should be fast and technically clean. This is speed training, not conditioning. Reduce or stop sprinting if the right groin becomes painful or increasingly aggravated.

- Acceleration: 3 x 10m | rest: sprint | notes: powerful first steps; accelerate through the line
- Acceleration: 3 x 20m | rest: sprint | notes: high intent; take enough recovery to preserve speed
- Sprint: 2 x 30m | rest: sprint | notes: smooth acceleration; do not strain for speed

### Adductor Preparation (recovery)

> Build capacity conservatively. This should feel controlled rather than provocative.

- Short-Lever Copenhagen Plank: 2 x 15-20s hold, each side | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: keep hips tall; use a pain-free range

### Lower Strength (strength)

> Train hard without grinding. Most working sets should finish around 1-3 RIR.

- Hack Squat: 3 x 6-10 | rest: heavy compound | notes: controlled depth; keep pressure through the whole foot
- Romanian Deadlift: 3 x 8-12 | rest: heavy compound | notes: push hips backward; maintain a stable torso; feel a controlled hamstring stretch
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 2 x 8-12 each side | rest: moderate compound | notes: use a stable stance; control the descent
- Seated Leg Curl / Lying Leg Curl: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: control the eccentric; avoid rushing repetitions
- Adductor Machine: 2 x 12-15 | rest: isolation | notes: controlled range; do not force painful range of motion
- Standing Calf Raise: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: pause in the stretched position; reach a strong contraction

### Abs (core)

> Abs are a hypertrophy priority. Add reps or resistance over time just as you would for any other muscle.

- Cable Crunch: 3 x 8-15 | rest: isolation | notes: curl ribs toward pelvis; do not simply hinge at the hips
- Hanging Knee Raise: 3 x 8-15 | rest: isolation | notes: finish by curling the pelvis upward; minimize swinging


## Wednesday: Back + Pulling Strength + Abs

Primary back-development day with extra emphasis on improving relative pulling strength.

Target duration: 65-70 minutes

+ Ultimate practice later

### Upper Body Warm-Up (warmup)

- Pulling Preparation: Prepare shoulders, scapulae, and upper back without creating fatigue. (~5-7 min)
  - Dead hang
  - Scapular pull-ups
  - Band pull-aparts
  - Light cable row

### Pulling Strength (strength)

> Pull-ups are both a back-building exercise and an athletic relative-strength benchmark. Use clean repetitions rather than chasing ugly reps.

- Pull-Up: 4 x 3-5 | rest: heavy compound | notes: begin from a controlled hang; drive elbows down; stop before technique deteriorates
- Chest-Supported Row: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound | notes: keep chest supported; pull through the elbows; control the stretch
- Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound | notes: drive elbows toward the hips; avoid excessive torso movement
- Single-Arm Cable Row: 2 x 10-15 each side | rest: isolation | notes: allow a controlled reach; pull elbow back toward the hip
- Reverse Pec Deck: 3 x 12-20 | rest: isolation | notes: move through the rear shoulders rather than shrugging
- Incline Dumbbell Curl: 3 x 8-12 | rest: isolation | notes: keep upper arm stable; control the stretched position
- Cable Curl: 2 x 12-15 | rest: isolation | notes: keep tension continuous; avoid swinging

### Abs (core)

- Ab Wheel: 3 x 6-12 | rest: isolation | notes: keep ribs down; extend only as far as you can control without excessive lower-back arching


## Thursday: Restore + Move + L-Sit

A deliberately low-fatigue session between Wednesday and Thursday Ultimate practices. The objective is to leave feeling better than when you arrived. There is no reward for using all 75 available minutes.

Target duration: 45-55 minutes

+ Ultimate practice later

### Easy Aerobic Warm-Up (cardio)

- Easy Cycling: Comfortable conversational cycling to increase blood flow. (~8-10 min)

### Mobility Flow (mobility)

> Use controlled, comfortable ranges. Do not aggressively stretch an irritated groin.

- Lower and Upper Body Mobility: Controlled mobility through commonly used athletic ranges. (~12-15 min)
  - 90/90 hip switches
  - Adductor rockbacks
  - Hip flexor mobility
  - Deep squat hold
  - Ankle dorsiflexion rocks
  - Thoracic rotations
  - Shoulder CARs
  - Wrist mobility

### Resilience and Control (recovery)

- Short-Lever Copenhagen Plank: 3 x 15-30s hold, each side | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: maintain stacked hips; progress conservatively
- Single-Leg Glute Bridge: 2 x 10-15 each side | rest: isolation | notes: keep pelvis level; finish with the glute
- Tibialis Raise: 2 x 15-20 | rest: isolation | notes: use controlled full repetitions
- Calf Raise: 2 x 15-20 | rest: isolation | notes: controlled stretch and contraction
- Scapular Pull-Up: 2 x 8-12 | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: keep elbows straight; move through the shoulder blades
- Dead Hang: 2 x 30-60s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: use a comfortable shoulder position

### L-Sit Skill (calisthenics)

> High-quality attempts only. Progress from tuck toward full L-sit over time.

- L-Sit Practice: Four to five high quality attempts using the hardest progression that can be held with good position. | notes: depress shoulders; keep arms straight; prioritize position over duration


## Friday: Athletic Power + Lower B + Abs

Develop explosiveness before strength work. Unlike Tuesday's linear acceleration emphasis, power work should eventually develop both vertical/horizontal and lateral qualities useful for Ultimate.

Target duration: 70-75 minutes

### Athletic Warm-Up (warmup)

- Dynamic Power Warm-Up: Prepare ankles, hips, knees, and nervous system for explosive jumping. (~8-10 min)
  - Easy movement
  - Leg swings
  - Ankle preparation
  - Glute activation
  - Low amplitude pogos
  - Two progressive jumps

### Elastic Power (power)

> Every repetition should be crisp and explosive. Take enough rest to maintain quality. Stop adding reps when jump performance noticeably deteriorates.

- Pogo Jump: 2 x 15-20 | rest: jump | notes: quick ground contacts; stay tall and elastic
- Box Jump: 3 x 3 | rest: jump | notes: jump explosively; use a box height that does not require excessive knee tucking
- Standing Broad Jump: 3 x 3 | rest: jump | notes: maximize quality and distance; reset fully between reps
- Lateral Bound: 2 x 4-6 each side | rest: jump | notes: push powerfully sideways; stick the landing under control

### Lower Strength (strength)

- Trap-Bar Deadlift: 3 x 5-8 | rest: heavy compound | notes: brace before each rep; drive the floor away; avoid grinding repetitions
- Leg Press: 3 x 8-12 | rest: heavy compound | notes: use controlled depth; keep hips stable
- Reverse Lunge: 2 x 8-12 each side | rest: moderate compound | notes: control the landing; drive through the front leg
- Seated Leg Curl / Lying Leg Curl: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: control both directions
- Hip Abduction: 2 x 12-20 | rest: isolation | notes: use controlled range rather than momentum
- Calf Raise: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: controlled stretch; strong finish

### Abs (core)

- Hanging Knee Raise / Hanging Leg Raise: 3 x 8-15 | rest: isolation | notes: progressively move toward straighter legs as strength improves; minimize swinging
- Cable Crunch: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: progressively overload while maintaining spinal flexion rather than hip hinging


## Saturday: Shoulders + Back + Arms + Planche

A hypertrophy-focused upper-body session emphasizing the physique priorities of shoulders and back while providing the second weekly planche exposure.

Target duration: 70-75 minutes

### Upper Body Warm-Up (warmup)

- Upper Body Preparation: Prepare wrists, shoulders, and scapulae for planche and upper-body training. (~6-8 min)
  - Wrist preparation
  - Scapular push-ups
  - Band pull-aparts
  - Light shoulder rotations

### Planche Skill (calisthenics)

> Planche is a long-term skill. Progress leverage and position gradually rather than chasing exhaustion.

- Planche Lean: 4 x 10-20s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: locked elbows; strong protraction; posterior pelvic tilt
- Pseudo-Planche Push-Up: 3 x 5-10 | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: hands near hips relative to a normal push-up; maintain forward lean and whole-body tension

### Shoulder and Back Hypertrophy (strength)

> Shoulders and back receive intentional extra volume because they are physique priorities.

- Machine Shoulder Press: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound | notes: use a stable setup; control the eccentric
- Pull-Up / Assisted Pull-Up: 3 x 5-8 | rest: heavy compound | notes: use assistance if necessary to keep every repetition clean
- Cable Lateral Raise: 4 x 12-20 | rest: isolation | notes: control the lowering phase; avoid torso momentum
- Seated Cable Row: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound | notes: control the stretch; pull through the elbows
- Rear-Delt Fly: 3 x 12-20 | rest: isolation | notes: keep tension on rear shoulders; avoid shrugging
- Cable Curl / Dumbbell Curl: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: full controlled repetitions
- Triceps Pressdown: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: keep upper arms stable; reach full comfortable extension

### Zone 2 Cardio (cardio, optional)

> Add this only when recovery from lifting and Ultimate is good. It should support aerobic development without making the legs substantially more fatigued.

- Easy Cycling: Comfortable conversational cycling. (~30-45 min)
