# Athletic Muscle and Calisthenics Foundation

## Monday: Upper Strength and Planche Foundation

Upper-body strength with planche and handstand practice. Frisbee later.

Target duration: 75 minutes

+ Frisbee practice later

### Warm-Up (warmup)

- Upper-Body Dynamic Warm-Up: Shoulder circles, band pull-aparts, wrist preparation, scapular push-ups, and light movement. (~8-10 min)

### Planche Foundation (calisthenics)

> Emphasize straight elbows, protracted shoulders, and a strong hollow-body position.

- Planche Lean: 4 x 10-20s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: lean only as far as you can control; push the floor away
- Pseudo-Planche Push-Up: 3 x 5-10 | rest: moderate compound | notes: keep elbows controlled and shoulders forward
- Wall Handstand Hold: 4 x 20-40s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: push tall through the shoulders

### Upper-Body Strength (strength)

- Pull-Up: 5 x 1-3 | rest: heavy compound | notes: stop before form breaks; use Assisted Pull-Up afterward
- Dumbbell Bench Press: 4 x 6-10 | rest: heavy compound
- Chest-Supported Row: 4 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Overhead Barbell Press: 3 x 6-10 | rest: heavy compound
- Dip: 3 x 5-8 | rest: moderate compound

### Shoulders and Core (core)

- Cable Lateral Raise: 4 x 12-20 | rest: isolation
- Hanging Knee Raise: 4 x 8-15 | rest: moderate compound
- Hollow Body Hold: 3 x 20-40s hold | rest: calisthenics skill

## Tuesday: Lower-Body Strength and Hypertrophy

Primary lower-body strength session. Keep one to three good repetitions in reserve.

Target duration: 75 minutes

### Warm-Up (warmup)

- Lower-Body Dynamic Warm-Up: Ankle preparation, leg swings, hip openers, bodyweight squats, and glute activation. (~8-10 min)

### Squat Strength (strength)

- Front Squat: 4 x 6-8 | rest: heavy compound | notes: brace hard and use controlled depth
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 3 x 8-12 each side | rest: moderate compound
- Leg Press: 3 x 10-15 | rest: moderate compound

### Posterior Chain (strength)

- Romanian Deadlift: 4 x 8-10 | rest: heavy compound | notes: maintain a long spine and controlled stretch
- Seated Leg Curl: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation

### Calves and Core (core)

- Standing Calf Raise: 5 x 10-15 | rest: isolation | notes: pause in the stretched and contracted positions
- Ab Wheel: 4 x 6-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Short-Lever Copenhagen Plank: 3 x 20-30s hold, each side | rest: isolation

### Mobility (mobility)

- Lower-Body Mobility Flow: Hips, ankles, hamstrings, and quadriceps. (~8-10 min)

## Wednesday: Pull Strength and Muscle-Up Foundation

Back-focused session with pulling strength, muscle-up preparation, and grip work. Frisbee later.

Target duration: 75 minutes

+ Frisbee practice later

### Warm-Up (warmup)

- Pulling Preparation: Scapular movement, band work, wrist preparation, and easy hanging. (~8-10 min)

### Muscle-Up Foundation (calisthenics)

> Build the strength and positions for a future muscle-up without repeatedly failing attempts.

- Scapular Pull-Up: 3 x 8-12 | rest: calisthenics skill
- False-Grip Hang: 4 x 10-20s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Assisted Muscle-Up Transition: 4 x 3-5 | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: use a low bar or resistance band
- Explosive Assisted Pull-Up: 4 x 3-5 | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: pull toward the chest

### Pulling Strength (strength)

- Pull-Up: 6 x 1-3 | rest: heavy compound | notes: accumulate clean reps
- Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Barbell Row: 4 x 6-10 | rest: heavy compound
- Single-Arm Cable Row: 3 x 8-12 each side | rest: moderate compound

### Rear Delts and Arms (strength)

- Face Pull: 4 x 12-20 | rest: isolation
- Incline Dumbbell Curl: 3 x 8-12 | rest: isolation
- Hammer Curl: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation

### Grip and Core (core)

- Dead Hang: 3 x 20-40s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Tuck L-Sit: 5 x 5-15s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Pallof Press: 3 x 10-15 each side | rest: isolation

## Thursday: Shoulders, Chest, Arms, and Handstand

A lower-fatigue upper-body hypertrophy and skill session. Frisbee later.

Target duration: 75 minutes

+ Frisbee practice later

### Warm-Up (warmup)

- Upper-Body Dynamic Warm-Up: Wrists, shoulders, thoracic spine, band pull-aparts, and scapular control. (~8-10 min)

### Handstand Foundation (calisthenics)

> Practice balance and shoulder elevation without training to failure.

- Wall-Facing Handstand Hold: 5 x 20-40s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Pike Push-Up: 4 x 6-12 | rest: moderate compound | notes: maintain a tall shoulder position
- Wall Handstand Shoulder Shrug: 3 x 8-12 | rest: calisthenics skill

### Chest Hypertrophy (strength)

- Incline Dumbbell Press: 4 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Ring Push-Up: 3 x 8-15 | rest: moderate compound
- Cable Fly: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation

### Shoulders and Arms (strength)

- Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Cable Lateral Raise: 5 x 12-20 | rest: isolation
- Rear-Delt Fly: 4 x 12-20 | rest: isolation
- Overhead Cable Triceps Extension: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation
- EZ-Bar Curl: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation

### Mobility (mobility)

- Shoulder, Wrist, and Thoracic Mobility: Controlled mobility flow. (~8-10 min)

## Friday: Athletic Lower Body, Calves, and Core

Lower-body athleticism, unilateral strength, stability, and trunk development.

Target duration: 75 minutes

### Warm-Up (warmup)

- Athletic Lower-Body Warm-Up: Ankle preparation, hip openers, skips, glute activation, and progressive movement. (~8-10 min)

### Power and Athleticism (power)

> Every repetition should be explosive and technically clean. Stop before fatigue reduces speed.

- Box Jump: 4 x 3 | rest: jump | notes: land quietly and reset between repetitions
- Standing Broad Jump: 3 x 3 | rest: jump | notes: prioritize quality over distance
- Medicine Ball Rotational Throw: 3 x 5 each side | rest: moderate compound

### Unilateral Strength (strength)

- Reverse Lunge: 4 x 8-12 each side | rest: moderate compound
- Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift: 3 x 8-10 each side | rest: moderate compound
- Goblet Squat: 3 x 10-15 | rest: moderate compound
- Hip Thrust: 4 x 8-12 | rest: heavy compound

### Lower Legs (strength)

- Seated Calf Raise: 5 x 12-20 | rest: isolation
- Tibialis Raise: 3 x 15-25 | rest: isolation

### Core Strength (core)

- Hanging Leg Raise: 4 x 6-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Side Plank: 3 x 30-45s hold, each side | rest: isolation
- Reverse Crunch: 3 x 12-20 | rest: isolation

### Mobility (mobility)

- Hip, Ankle, and Hamstring Mobility: Controlled mobility flow. (~8-10 min)

## Saturday: Calisthenics Skill and Full-Body Hypertrophy

Skill-focused session with moderate hypertrophy work. Keep this session controlled so it does not interfere with long-run recovery.

Target duration: 75 minutes

### Warm-Up (warmup)

- Full-Body Dynamic Warm-Up: Wrists, shoulders, hips, ankles, scapular movement, and light locomotion. (~8-10 min)

### Calisthenics Skill Circuit (calisthenics)

> Perform high-quality attempts. Stop every set before technical failure.

- Wall Handstand Hold: 4 x 20-45s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Planche Lean: 3 x 10-20s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Tuck Flag Hold: 4 x 5-15s hold, each side | rest: calisthenics skill
- Tuck L-Sit: 5 x 5-20s hold | rest: calisthenics skill
- Assisted Pull-Up: 4 x 6-10 | rest: calisthenics skill
- Ring Support Hold: 4 x 15-30s hold | rest: calisthenics skill

### Full-Body Hypertrophy (strength)

- Machine Chest Press: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Machine Row: 3 x 8-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Leg Extension: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation
- Lying Leg Curl: 3 x 10-15 | rest: isolation
- Dumbbell Lateral Raise: 3 x 15-25 | rest: isolation

### Core Finisher (core)

- Ab Wheel: 3 x 6-12 | rest: moderate compound
- Hollow Body Hold: 3 x 20-40s hold | rest: isolation

### Full-Body Mobility (mobility)

- Full-Body Flexibility Flow: Shoulders, wrists, thoracic spine, hips, hamstrings, calves, and ankles. (~12-15 min)
