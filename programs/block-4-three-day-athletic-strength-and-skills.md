# Three-Day Strength, Calisthenics and Mobility

## Monday: Upper Body, Handstands and Planche
Repeat this weekly template; review recovery and progress every 4-6 weeks. Most strength sets finish at 2-3 RIR. Use controlled 1-2 second lifting and 2-3 second lowering phases, except explosive power work. Increase load only after every set reaches the top of its range with sound technique.
This is the baseline for your current running build. During December 7-13, halve strength and core sets, rounding up; during race week, skip lower-body lifting and keep upper-body work optional and light. Resume the baseline after post-ultra recovery. The practice flag applies only while Ultimate is scheduled; your last pre-race practice is November 19.
Target duration: 60 minutes
+ Ultimate practice later

### Wrist and Shoulder Preparation (warmup)
> Allow about 5 minutes including easy rehearsal sets for the first strength exercises.
- Wrist Preparation: 1 x 60-90s | notes: gentle wrist rocks and comfortable wrist circles
- Scapular Push-Up: 1 x 8-12 | notes: easy preparation; elbows stay straight
- Kettlebell Halo: 1 x 5-8 each side | notes: each side means each direction; use a light bell; smooth circles close around the head

### Handstand and Planche Skill (calisthenics)
> Allow 8-10 minutes. Rest sufficiently for quality; stop before form deteriorates. Use a handstand setup you can enter and exit safely.
- Wall Handstand Hold: 3 x 15-30s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: gradually increase clean hold time; then reduce wall assistance rather than extending holds indefinitely
- Planche Lean: 2 x 8-15s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: elbows straight; push the floor away; begin with a small lean; increase lean slightly after both holds reach 15 seconds comfortably

### Upper Body Strength (strength)
> In each superset, move from the first exercise to the second, then take the prescribed rest. Rest guidance on the second exercise applies to the whole pair.
- Floor Press: 3 x 6-10 each side | superset: A | notes: single-arm kettlebell version; keep ribs controlled; perform light rehearsal sets first
- Pull-Up: 3 x 4-8 | superset: A | rest: heavy compound | notes: use assistance as needed to retain 2-3 RIR; reduce assistance after reaching the top of the range
- Cable Lateral Raise: 2 x 12-20 each side | superset: B | notes: controlled comfortable range; no torso swing
- Face Pull: 2 x 12-20 | superset: B | rest: isolation | notes: smooth pull toward eye level; avoid shrugging

### Abs and Loaded Carry (core)
- Ab Wheel: 2 x 5-8 | rest: isolation | notes: kneeling version; stop before the lower back sags; build repetitions, then extend rollout distance gradually
- Suitcase Carry: 2 x 30-40s each side | rest: moderate compound | notes: stay upright with ribs over pelvis; increase load after both sets reach 40 seconds without leaning

### Shoulder and Thoracic Mobility (mobility)
- Mobility Flow: Controlled comfortable mobility. (~6-8 min)
  - Open Book Rotation: 6-8 each side
  - Wall Slide: 8-10 controlled repetitions
  - Gentle lat stretch: 20-30 seconds each side
  - Doorway chest stretch: 20-30 seconds each side

## Tuesday: Pistol Skill, Lower Body Strength and Power
Separate this session from your easy run by several hours when practical. Keep leg training at 3 RIR during the ultra build; start unfamiliar lower-body exercises with one working set in the first week. Reduce sets if soreness affects subsequent easy running. No strength sets should be taken to failure.
Target duration: 60 minutes

### Hip and Ankle Preparation (warmup)
- Dynamic Warm-Up: Unloaded squat and hinge rehearsals, ankle rocks and comfortable hip movements. (~3-4 min)
- Hip Abduction: 1 x 10-15 each side | rest: isolation | notes: light mini-band above knees; standing supported or side-lying version; keep pelvis steady

### Low-Fatigue Power (power)
- Medicine Ball Chest Pass: 2 x 3-5 | rest: jump | notes: use a light ball and safe throwing area; explosive release; recover fully between sets; stop when speed drops

### Pistol Squat Skill (calisthenics)
- Pistol Squat: 2 x 3-5 each side | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: use secure hand support and an appropriately high box; keep repetitions smooth and easy; lower the box or reduce assistance only after both sets reach 5 clean repetitions

### Lower Body Strength (strength)
- Goblet Squat: 3 x 6-10 | rest: moderate compound | notes: comfortable full range; stable feet and controlled descent; 3 RIR during the running build
- Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift: 2 x 6-8 each side | rest: moderate compound | notes: light fingertip support and a kettlebell in the opposite hand; hips move backward; keep hips square; stop at your controlled range
- Seated Leg Curl: 2 x 10-15 | superset: C | notes: controlled knee flexion and return; avoid forcing end range
- Adductor Machine: 2 x 12-15 | superset: C | rest: isolation | notes: light initial load and comfortable range; no pushing through groin pain; persistent or worsening symptoms warrant sports medicine or physical therapy assessment
- Standing Calf Raise: 2 x 10-15 | superset: D | notes: controlled rise and lowering; comfortable range; no bouncing
- Tibialis Raise: 2 x 12-20 | superset: D | rest: isolation | notes: light tibialis bar or suitable band resistance; keep heels supported; increase resistance after all repetitions are controlled; this is strengthening, not a guarantee against shin pain

### Hip and Ankle Mobility (mobility)
- Mobility Flow: Move slowly through comfortable ranges. (~7-9 min)
  - Ankle Dorsiflexion Rock: 8-10 each side
  - 90/90 Hip Switch: 6-8 controlled repetitions
  - Adductor Rock Back: 6-8 each side
  - Hip Flexor Stretch: 30-40 seconds each side

## Thursday: Handstands, L-Sit and Rotational Strength
Keep the session controlled so you remain fresh for evening practice. Use the scheduled practice flag only while practices are active. This is upper-body and core training; do not turn the rotational work into an exhausting circuit.
Target duration: 60 minutes
+ Ultimate practice later

### Wrist and Shoulder Preparation (warmup)
> Allow about 5 minutes including light rehearsal sets before pressing and rowing.
- Wrist Preparation: 1 x 60-90s | notes: gentle wrist rocks and comfortable wrist circles
- Kettlebell Around the World: 1 x 6-8 each side | notes: each side means each direction; pass a light bell around the waist between hands; keep torso steady and handle transfers controlled
- Wall Slide: 1 x 8-10 | notes: comfortable shoulder range; avoid arching the lower back

### Handstand and L-Sit Skill (calisthenics)
> Allow 8-10 minutes. Stop before fatigue changes your position.
- Wall Handstand Hold: 3 x 15-30s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: build clean hold time; gradually reduce wall assistance when control allows
- Tuck L-Sit: 2 x 8-15s hold | rest: calisthenics skill | notes: use parallettes; press shoulders down; after both holds reach 15 seconds, gradually extend one leg rather than lengthening holds indefinitely

### Upper Body Strength (strength)
- Landmine Press: 3 x 8-12 each side | superset: E | notes: half-kneeling version; keep ribs over pelvis; use a securely anchored bar
- Seated Cable Row: 3 x 8-12 | superset: E | rest: moderate compound | notes: neutral grip; pull toward lower ribs; allow controlled shoulder-blade movement; avoid rocking backward
- Dip: 2 x 6-10 | rest: heavy compound | notes: use assistance as needed; comfortable shoulder range; retain 2-3 RIR

### Rotational Core and Abs (core)
- Landmine Rotation: 2 x 6-8 each side | rest: moderate compound | notes: start light with a secure landmine attachment; pivot feet and hips smoothly with the bar; each side means a controlled sweep toward that side; increase load only after both sets are clean at the top of the range
- Hanging Knee Raise: 2 x 6-10 | rest: isolation | notes: curl pelvis upward without swinging; add repetitions, then gradually straighten legs to increase difficulty

### Upper Body and Hip Mobility (mobility)
- Mobility Flow: Gentle mobility with relaxed breathing. (~6-8 min)
  - Open Book Rotation: 6-8 each side
  - Wall Slide: 8-10 controlled repetitions
  - Gentle lat stretch: 20-30 seconds each side
  - Hip Flexor Stretch: 30-40 seconds each side
