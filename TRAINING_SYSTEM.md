## 10. Hypertrophy Execution Rules

The athlete is still improving lifting technique.

Priority:

**Stability → Range of Motion → Control → Load**

General tempo guidance:

### Concentric
Approximately 1–2 seconds.

### Eccentric
Approximately 2–3 seconds.

Use controlled, repeatable technique.

A dramatic muscle sensation is not required for hypertrophy, but exercises should be performed in a manner that appropriately loads the intended musculature.

---

## 11. RIR

Use Reps In Reserve.

Examples:

4 RIR = approximately four additional reps possible.

2 RIR = approximately two additional reps possible.

1 RIR = approximately one additional rep possible.

0 RIR = no additional repetition possible with appropriate technique.

Most hypertrophy work should generally finish around:

# 1–3 RIR

Failure is not required on every set.

---

## 12. Double Progression

Primary strength/hypertrophy progression method:

# DOUBLE PROGRESSION

Example:

Prescription:

3 × 6–10

Session A:

25 × 9  
25 × 8  
25 × 7

Next exposure:

Attempt to improve repetitions while preserving technique.

Eventually:

25 × 10  
25 × 10  
25 × 10

Then increase load.

Return toward the lower portion of the rep range.

Do not sacrifice technique merely to achieve progression.

## 13. Rest Periods

General guidelines:

### Heavy compound exercises
Approximately 2–3 minutes.

### Moderate compound exercises
Approximately 90–150 seconds.

### Isolation exercises
Approximately 60–120 seconds.

### Sprinting
Long enough to preserve speed quality.

### Jumps
Long enough to preserve explosiveness.

### Calisthenics skill
Long enough to produce high-quality attempts.

The app may provide timers, but timers should remain optional.

---

## 14. Abs

Abs are a high-priority physique goal.

Train them like other muscles.

Use:

- resistance
- controlled ROM
- progressive overload
- measurable progression

Primary movements currently include:

- cable crunch
- hanging knee/leg raise
- ab wheel
- hollow-body work
- L-sit work

Avoid replacing this with arbitrary high-repetition "ab burner" circuits.

---

## 15. Athleticism Rules

Muscle gain must coexist with athletic development.

The program deliberately maintains:

### Sprint exposure
Tuesday + Ultimate.

### Power exposure
Friday + Ultimate.

### Change of direction
Ultimate.

### Relative strength
Pull-ups, dips, calisthenics.

### Mobility
Integrated throughout week.

### Conditioning
Ultimate + optional Zone 2.

The athlete does NOT need additional random "functional" exercises merely to appear athletic.

Stable hypertrophy exercises are useful for building muscle.

Dedicated athletic exercises are useful for developing athletic qualities.

Each tool should serve its intended purpose.

---

## 16. Speed Philosophy

Speed training is not conditioning.

Speed repetitions should occur relatively fresh.

Use:

- high intent
- excellent technique
- adequate recovery
- low/moderate volume

Do not turn sprint training into repeated exhausted running.

Ultimate already provides substantial repeated-sprint conditioning.

---

## 17. Power Philosophy

Power work occurs before heavy strength work.

Jumps should be:

- explosive
- technically sound
- relatively low volume

Stop or reduce volume when performance meaningfully deteriorates.

---

## 18. Relative Strength

Bodyweight gain is not automatically beneficial.

The objective is:

**more muscle + substantially greater strength + maintained/improved speed and power**

rather than:

**more mass regardless of performance**

Important benchmarks include:

- strict pull-ups
- dips
- sprint performance
- jumping
- calisthenics

---

### 10 m sprint

Acceleration.

### 20 m sprint

Acceleration.

### 30 m sprint

Speed.

### Standing broad jump

Horizontal power.

### Vertical jump

Vertical power.

### Strict pull-ups

Relative pulling strength.

### Strict dips

Relative pushing strength.

### L-sit

Body control / compression strength.

### Planche progression

Straight-arm strength and skill.

Benchmark testing should not unnecessarily interfere with normal training.

---

## 20. Adductor / Groin Management

The program includes gradual strengthening through movements such as:

- Copenhagen plank
- adductor machine
- unilateral leg work
- glute work
- controlled mobility

The software must distinguish:

**ordinary training soreness**

from

**pain or recurring aggravation.**

The software must not diagnose the cause of groin symptoms.

If symptoms:

- worsen
- persist
- affect sprinting
- affect cutting
- produce significant pain

recommend assessment by an appropriate sports medicine professional or physical therapist.

---

## 21. Program Adjustment Philosophy

Do not modify the training plan simply because one workout was poor.

Look for trends.

Potential reasons for modification:

- persistent poor recovery
- declining performance
- repeated inability to complete prescribed volume
- worsening pain
- Ultimate workload changes
- athletic benchmark regression
- sustained strength plateau
- schedule changes

The program should evolve based on evidence.

---

## 22. Future HYROX Phase

HYROX is intentionally excluded from current specific programming.

A future training phase can introduce:

- compromised running
- sled work
- HYROX stations
- event-specific pacing
- race-specific conditioning

Do not introduce these simply because HYROX is a future goal.

The current block develops useful prerequisites.
